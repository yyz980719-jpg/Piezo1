Interpretation: the reconstruction is on the SAME SCALE as an independent
whole-blood eQTL resource (ratio of median magnitudes 0.57-0.79, i.e. within
~1.3-1.8 fold). It is not off by an order of magnitude. Direction agreement is
complete among well-powered variants and incomplete for weaker signals, which is
expected given GTEx n=670 at a locus with more than one signal.

Consequence for the manuscript: the MR null is scale-INVARIANT (IVW z is
unchanged by any rescaling of the exposure), so the null conclusion is unaffected.
Only the SD-unit effect estimate and the MDE/power statement are scale-dependent;
these are reported with an explicit scale-error sensitivity table.
