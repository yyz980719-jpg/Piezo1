# Third-party data and software

Selected-cell research inputs derive from publicly deposited GEO records:

- GSE255460: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE255460 ; source publication https://doi.org/10.1136/ard-2023-224420 .
- GSE220243: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE220243 ; source publication https://doi.org/10.1136/ard-2022-223227 .

Preserve the original accession, donor/sample crosswalks and source attribution. Source annotations are inherited labels, not newly validated functional states. Identifiers in this release are the public-source research identifiers, not a claim of access to identifiable clinical records.

NCBI states that it places no restrictions on use or distribution of GEO data, while warning that submitters may claim intellectual-property rights: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html . This release does not override source-owner rights or guarantee suitability for a particular use.

NumPy, pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib and Pillow retain their respective licenses. They are dependencies, not redistributed binaries. The repository MIT license applies to original code, not automatically to external data, published text, software dependencies or source annotations. No unpublished main or supplementary manuscript is included.
